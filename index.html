K2-Linux Meta-Distribution

A source-based, highly configurable Linux distribution designed for total control. No bloat, no installer scripts, absolute control over your toolchain and kernel compilation.

root@k2-live # fetch https://distfiles.k2linux.org/stages/stage3-amd64.tar.xz

01. Installation Handbook

Follow the official manual to compile and configure your custom K2-Linux environment from scratch.

1. Preparing the Disks Partitioning (GPT/MBR), LUKS encryption, and filesystems (EXT4/Btrfs). 2. Unpacking Stage Tarball Setting up chroot, selecting mirrors, and configuring make.conf. 3. Kernel Configuration Manual kernel compilation with menuconfig and driver selection. 4. System Bootstrapping Installing init system, bootloader (GRUB/EFISTUB), and essential tools.
